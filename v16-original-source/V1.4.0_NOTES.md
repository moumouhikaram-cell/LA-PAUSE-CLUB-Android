# LA PAUSE CLUB Manager v1.4.0

Business release for real room operations.

- Dynamic pricing engine: time, budget, configurable formulas, prepaid free-credit mode.
- Default payment policy: always before play; extensions/reloads prepaid.
- Full snack & drink POS with real branded product imagery, stock and payment methods.
- Cash accounting separates revenue, cash injection/change, expenses, expected cash, counted cash and closing difference.
- Tournament workflow: registration, check-in, qualifications, knockout, final, winner.
- Challenges: live rankings, close/reopen, permanent winner display.
- Critical client backup in native SharedPreferences in addition to full state backup.
- Rotation keeps the current page and drill-down; swipe left returns one navigation level.
- 6 PS5 grid + full-width SIM RACING VIP card.
- KPI blocks stay fully visible without horizontal scrolling.
- Background native alerts: warning, end, critical overdue; restored after boot/app replacement.
- Dynamic image renderer from v1.3.2 retained.
